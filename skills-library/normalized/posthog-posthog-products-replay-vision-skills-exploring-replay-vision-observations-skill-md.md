# Résumé
Guides agents through pulling a Replay Vision scanner's observations, reading the findings, and acting on them — summarizing patterns across sessions, drilling into individual recordings, and turning real, corroborated issues into PostHog tasks, insights, or an investigating-replay hand-off.
TRIGGER when: user wants to pull/read/triage Replay Vision observations, asks "what has my scanner found", wants to act on or summarize scanner findings, turn observations into tasks/work, or points at a /replay-vision/<scanner-id> URL.
DO NOT TRIGGER when: creating or sizing a scanner (use creating-replay-vision-scanners), running a one-off scan you don't then analyse, or authoring a signals scout.
