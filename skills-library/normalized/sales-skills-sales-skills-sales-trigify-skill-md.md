# Résumé
Trigify platform help — AI agent-powered LinkedIn signal intelligence and social listening for B2B GTM teams with custom AI workflows, engagement tracking, competitor monitoring, CRM integrations (HubSpot, Attio, Apollo), outreach sequencing (Instantly, HeyReach, Smartlead), and the Jarvis AI workflow builder. Use when Trigify searches return too many low-intent LinkedIn signals and you need to tune keywords, AI workflows aren't routing signals to the right CRM or outreach tool, credit consumption burns through your quota too fast, you want a workflow that enriches LinkedIn engagers and pushes them to your sequence, CRM sync with HubSpot or Attio keeps breaking, Social Actions on X aren't engaging the right posts, Jarvis AI built a workflow that doesn't match what you described, or you're comparing Trigify vs Clearcue vs Octolens vs CatchIntent for LinkedIn signal intelligence. Do NOT use for social listening strategy across tools or choosing between social listening platforms (use /sales-social-listening).
